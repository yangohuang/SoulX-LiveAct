# Two-step sampling on one RTX 4090: speed versus lip synchronization

The [prospective gate](../superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-two-step-quality-frontier.md) asked whether removing the middle denoising step could give a defensible speed/quality trade-off at the same 416×720 resolution. **It did not pass.** The matched two-step run reduced median later-block time by 30.4%, but SyncNet confidence fell by **0.612**, more than the preregistered 0.30 allowance. The result is a useful sampling frontier, not a quality-preserving default or a real-time system. Per the gate, no image-1 or image-3 holdout was run.

## Matched runs

Both runs used image 2 (`examples/image/2.png`), seed 43, prompt `一个人在说话`, the same 30-second 16-kHz PCM audio (SHA-256 `85372c99f11ca8396d9c01bb1aa1314bb165e93000938ba87f8ad06de104637e`), 416×720 at 24 FPS, FP8 GEMM/KV, CPU block/cache offload, `resident_kv_steps=0`, cached T5/FP8 weights, and no compile or latent anchor. They were freshly generated in the same checkout and each decoded to 722 frames plus 30.000 seconds of audio. The three-step schedule has two audio-conditioned DiT calls; dropping timestep 937.5 leaves one in the two-step schedule. Thus this comparison changes the algorithm as well as the amount of computation. Archived [three-step request](artifacts/2026-09-25-image2-frontier-3step-request.json) and [two-step request](artifacts/2026-09-25-image2-frontier-2step-request.json) SHA-256 values were `fabff4062fffad01bb1881fa90be8bed277757e9cc49623306dbb15f9232e806` and `7d9fb62e945bef51d096ff8684771ea7e85862b32a6671d6307dbe4f2f1273d0`, respectively; they differ only in output path.

## Decision and research significance

Reject the unmodified two-step schedule as the quality-preserving path. Even its **2.095 block-only FPS** is over 11× below 24-FPS real-time output, before setup and encoding; merely cutting one step cannot close that gap on this 4090 configuration. A useful next optimization experiment should preserve both audio-conditioned steps and target measured weight/KV transfers or compute; a sampling change would need a new quality-gated schedule or distillation, not an unsupported speed claim. This exercise directly addresses the Vivix JD's diffusion-step acceleration question with measured speed, lip quality, motion, identity proxy and worst-window review. It does not show long-horizon degradation, solve interaction latency, or justify a SoulX quality PR.

**Later reproducibility audit:** [The fixed-seed investigation](2026-09-25-fixed-seed-determinism-diagnostic.md) found that the default cuDNN benchmark setting can make Wav2Vec and VAE conditioning differ across independent same-seed processes. This pair therefore supports an **observed** 0.612 SyncNet gate failure, but cannot attribute the full score gap solely to the removed denoising step. Re-run both arms with `--disable_cudnn_benchmark` before making a causal quality claim or reconsidering holdouts.

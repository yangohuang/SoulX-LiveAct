# From LiveAct boundary diagnosis to a trainable generated-history probe

## Why this experiment was run

LiveAct's 4090 rollout shows a visible pose jump near source frames 373→374. The block-12 transition is already unusually large in the DiT's first clean-latent prediction (cross-block RMSE 0.358 versus 0.156 and 0.209 for the adjacent pairs), and remains large at the final latent (0.434 versus 0.156 and 0.307). Re-decoding with longer VAE history does not remove it. Inference-only anchors reduce some adjacent-frame differences but can delay the same hand motion or hurt lip-sync. A 101,536-parameter frozen-backbone bridge learned synthetic corruption but did not remove the real pose jump and changed clean held-out windows. Those results motivate learning under the generator's own history, but do not demonstrate that forcing is the cause-specific fix.

## What the 4090 training probe actually proves

On the official Self-Forcing causal Wan 1.3B implementation at source revision `33593df`, a separate local probe loaded the Wan 1.3B base weights in bf16, froze them, added 737,280 rank-4 Q/V LoRA parameters, generated six three-frame history blocks without gradients, re-noised and cached their detached outputs, and backpropagated through a seventh three-frame block. At latent 60×104, both seeds 0 and 1 completed one AdamW step on the local RTX 4090 with finite nonzero gradients. Peak CUDA reserved memory was **18.68 GiB**; six history blocks took about **3.08 s** and last-block forward/backward about **1.02 s**. Code, tests and per-run JSON are on the personal fork branch [`yangohuang/Self-Forcing:feat/4090-rollout-feasibility`](https://github.com/yangohuang/Self-Forcing/tree/feat/4090-rollout-feasibility), commit `910c974`.

This is a **feasibility lower bound**, not a forcing-quality result. The Wan weights used for this first probe are the bidirectional base checkpoint loaded into the causal model, conditioning embeddings and regression targets are synthetic, and the loss is plain MSE. The published Self-Forcing DMD recipe also needs a real/fake score setup and a trained ODE initialization; the probe is not that recipe. The Wan 1.3B memory figure cannot be extrapolated to LiveAct's 18B DiT or to a full differentiable LiveAct rollout. In particular, it does not fix or retest LiveAct frame 373→374.

## Decision and next gates

1. **Transferable training feasibility:** The 4090 can execute a real causal KV-cache generated-history path and one LoRA backward step at the published Wan spatial latent size and 21-frame window. This falsifies the blanket claim that a single 4090 cannot run *any* generated-history training.
2. **Algorithmic quality gate tested:** The released ODE initialization and real UMT5 conditioning were used in a three-seed, matched target-history/generated-history LoRA comparison. Generated-history training reduced 21-frame held-out self-context flow MSE by only 0.000410 on average (about 0.24%), while target-context error rose 0.000437; 48-frame results and latent seam ratios were mixed. Two paired 7.875-second videos show no clear recovery of later-frame clarity. The [full report](/home/yg/yg/code/github/Self-Forcing/docs/benchmarks/2026-09-25-generated-history-ab.md) and [contact sheet](/home/yg/yg/code/github/Self-Forcing/docs/benchmarks/artifacts/context-ab-seed0-contact.png) document the result. This **fails** the preregistered quality gate. Wan had text only, not LiveAct's image/audio condition, and the one-future flow target is an imperfect proxy for on-policy video generation.
3. **LiveAct-specific gate:** A direct 18B adapter experiment would need aligned image/audio conditions and a distributional or otherwise valid generated-trajectory target before its 4090 memory, time and gradient path are measured. A Wan success is not a LiveAct implementation; the current small-model proxy gave no reason to integrate a forcing patch. Any candidate should target the *first predicted clean latent at real block boundaries*, where the LiveAct discrepancy has already been localized, and retain the same held-out identity and lip-sync checks used in the boundary study.
4. **Contribution gate:** Keep the current 4090 inference PR-ready branch independent. Do not merge the existing bridge or call the current anchor “forcing.” An official quality PR requires a repeatable improvement on actual LiveAct videos, a controlled default-off integration, and a review of video and lip-sync artifacts. No official PR is opened by this work.

## Accurate interview statement

“I profiled and ran an 18B streaming digital-human generator on one 4090, then localized a long-video pose jump to the first clean-latent prediction at a chunk boundary. I tested VAE-history and inference-anchor hypotheses and found their tradeoffs. To test a training alternative within the hardware limit, I built a causal Wan 1.3B LoRA probe: six detached generated blocks plus one trainable block fit in 18.68 GiB. A later three-seed target-history/generated-history comparison with the official causal initialization gave only a tiny short-window proxy gain, a target-history cost, and no consistent 48-frame or visible quality improvement. That is useful negative evidence, not a solved LiveAct quality issue or an SRF reproduction.”

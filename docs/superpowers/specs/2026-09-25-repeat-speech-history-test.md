# Repeated-speech long-history test

Question: Does the late SyncNet decline in an unanchored 90-second causal LiveAct rollout persist when all three 30-second periods contain *exactly the same source speech samples*?

Use the existing first-30-second identity-2 PCM speech segment. Repeat its samples three times without a gap or crossfade and verify that each segment is bitwise identical. Generate one 90-second causal rollout with identity 2, prompt, seed 43, 416×720/24 FPS, three denoising steps, FP8 GEMM/KV, offload, one resident KV step and no anchor. The archived [independent 30-second baseline](../../benchmarks/artifacts/videos/image2-baseline-seed43-30s.mp4) already used the **same exact PCM file** (SHA-256 `85372c99f11ca8396d9c01bb1aa1314bb165e93000938ba87f8ad06de104637e`), seed and runtime settings, so it serves as the fresh-history control without another GPU run. Keep source audio, request and video hashes. The 90-second trajectory receives the same speech at three elapsed-time positions; the 30-second trajectory tests whether the early score is reproduced under a separate process/history. Separately generated frames are not pixel-aligned counterfactuals. The subsequent default-off skip-last flag has no effect when anchor strength is zero.

Primary endpoint: SyncNet relative confidence computed from one continuous 25-FPS face track's 31-shift distance matrix in three equal 30-second bins. Require each bin to have at least 90% of 750 scored windows and report best offset, min distance, face-track length and decoded audio samples. A prespecified sensitivity check excludes two seconds at both edges of each repeated period, limiting audio-encoder and crop-boundary context effects. A late-minus-early confidence decrease of at least 0.5 in both full and interior bins, with best offset unchanged and a fresh-history score within 0.3 of the long-run early score, is a **candidate history/time signal**, not proof of causal exposure bias. Also inspect lip frames at matched phonetic moments, a coarse fixed-ROI mouth-motion proxy, 22/23/22 boundary-window motion/peak distributions, and once-per-second face detection/cosine. If motion collapses by over 10%, face coverage drops below 90%, or the mouth review shows different pose/visibility, label the signal confounded. A single seed remains exploratory; if the candidate signal and visible lip review both pass, replicate at seed 44 before promoting it. Otherwise stop and state the negative/ambiguous result.

The repeated waveform removes gross source-content variation, but the implementation applies SoX `tempo=25/24` to the *whole* waveform and Wav2Vec encodes the resulting whole sequence. A pre-result audit found that equal raw-PCM periods do **not** remain sample-identical after global SoX tempo (short-window cross-correlation peaks only around 0.28–0.30 after a few milliseconds of lag). Therefore audit the actual Wav2Vec DiT-conditioning tensors over interior repeated periods before any history attribution; require mean per-frame cosine at least 0.99 and 10th-percentile cosine at least 0.95 to call them approximately matched. If not met, treat the repeated-raw-audio result as confounded and design an exact-embedding-repeat intervention separately. Positional conditioning, initial noise and generated history still vary. A comparison with fresh history is an additional control, not an intervention on hidden state. Avoid claims of realtime inference, Self-Forcing training, or a resolved LiveAct defect.
